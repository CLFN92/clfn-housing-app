Health & Safety Risk Levels
Severe / Imminent (15 pts)
Immediate risk to life or health. Examples: active mould with respiratory symptoms, condemned structure, no heat in winter, raw sewage, electrical hazard causing injury risk. Requires public health order or signed inspection report.
Moderate (10 pts)
Significant hazard requiring prompt attention. Examples: major mould without acute symptoms, failing heating system, significant water intrusion, rodent infestation, broken windows in cold weather. Requires documented inspection.
Minor (5 pts)
Documented hazard that poses a risk but is not immediately dangerous. Examples: minor mould, small water leaks, aging electrical, pest evidence. Requires inspection documentation.
None (0 pts)
No documented health or safety hazard in the current dwelling.
Verified by inspection report or public health order. Tap ? for details on each level.

The NOS defines the appropriate number of bedrooms for a household. A dwelling is considered overcrowded when the number of people exceeds what the NOS allows for that bedroom count.
