The sovereignty promise

Your members' information belongs to your nation. Full stop.

Most software pools every customer's records into one big shared database. We don't. Home Land Homes is built so that each nation's data is genuinely, structurally its own.

A database of your own

Every nation gets its own separate database — not a shared one with a filter. Your applications, tenant files and finances are physically isolated from every other community's.

Aligned with OCAP® principles

Ownership, Control, Access and Possession guide how the platform is built: your nation decides who sees what, and your records stay in your possession — including if you ever choose to leave.

Never sold. Never mined.

Your data is not sold, shared, or used to train anything. There are no advertising trackers and no Big-Tech data harvesting — even the maps use OpenStreetMap instead of Google.

Hosted in Canada

Your nation's data is hosted on Canadian infrastructure and stays in Canada — not routed through wherever a vendor's servers happen to be cheapest.

What it does

Everything your housing department runs on, in one place.

Not generic property software with the labels changed — the workflows are modelled on how First Nations housing departments actually operate.

Applications & placement

  • Fair, transparent waitlists — needs-based scoring you can tune, with clear approval steps instead of arguments.
  • Real FN application types — new housing, transfers, and file updates handled differently, the way they should be.
  • Smart matching — match approved applicants to vacant units by configurable priority: on-reserve status, urgency, unit fit, Elders units.
  • BCR ineligibility handling — band council resolutions are flagged automatically at approval and assignment time.

Homes & tenants

  • Full unit inventory — every home with its condition, funding source, inspection dates and history.
  • Tenant Information Card — one card per tenant: agreement, notes, utilities, documents and unit history together.
  • Funding-aware records — CMHC Section 95, Section 10, Band Housing, Band Rep and FNCFS tracked per unit and renovation.
  • Inspections with photos — room-by-room condition reports that can spawn work orders on the spot.

Maintenance & renovations

  • Maintenance requests to done — from report to work order to sign-off, assigned to your own crew or a contractor.
  • QR-code requests — tenants scan a code and submit an issue with photos from their phone. No phone tag.
  • Renovation scopes & tendering — guided scope-of-work builder, RFQs, bids, awards and generated contracts.
  • Contractor registry — approvals, insurance and WSIB expiry tracked so nothing slips.

Money & accountability

  • Rent & arrears without the spreadsheet — ledger, payment arrangements, collections, loans, invoices and utilities.
  • Chief & Council dashboard — read-only KPIs for leadership; the underlying data never leaves the nation.
  • Audit trail on everything — every change is logged and tamper-resistant, so answers are minutes away, not meetings away.
  • Role-based permissions — staff see exactly what their role needs, from field crew to Executive Director.

For tenants & applicants

  • Apply from anywhere — members apply, update their file, or request a transfer online, with document upload.
  • No passwords to forget — secure magic-link sign-in by email.
  • Automatic updates — email notifications keep applicants and tenants informed at each step.

Staff superpowers

  • An AI assistant that knows your data — staff ask plain-language questions and get answers from your nation's own records. Read-only, permission-aware, and it never trains on your data.
  • Generated documents — occupancy agreements, work orders, contracts and reports produced as clean PDFs.
  • Works in the field — phone-friendly screens for crews doing inspections and work orders on site.

How it works

From first call to fully running, without a big-bang IT project.

  1. We set up your nation

    Your own isolated workspace is created — your database, your subdomain, your branding, your role structure.

  2. Your records come with you

    Existing units, tenants and waitlists are brought in from spreadsheets and paper files, together with your team.

  3. Staff and tenants get their tools

    Office staff, field crews, leadership and tenants each get the view that fits them — on desktop and phone, online or off.

  4. Your data stays yours

    You control access, you can export everything, and if you ever leave, your records go with you. That's the deal.

Trust & security

Boring, careful engineering — the kind you want holding your members' files.

  • Isolation by architecture

    One database per nation. A misconfiguration in someone else's workspace can't touch yours, because there is no shared data store to leak from.

  • Access on a need-to-know basis

    Role-based permissions down to individual actions: who can approve, who can assign, who can only view. Your ED configures it; the system enforces it.

  • An audit trail that can't be edited

    Every meaningful action is written to an append-only log — entries can't be quietly changed or deleted, by anyone.

  • Built for northern connectivity

    Slow or dropped connection in the field? Work is saved locally and synced when the connection returns — staff don't lose an inspection to a dead zone.

  • Canadian-hosted

    Data is hosted on infrastructure located in Canada. No offshore replication of your members' records.

  • Private by default

    No advertising trackers, no analytics scripts phoning home, no third-party cookies — on the product, and on this website.
