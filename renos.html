Terms of Reference

Constance Lake First Nation — Housing Department

1. Scope of Engagement

The contractor agrees to perform only the work described in the approved Scope of Work (SOW) issued by the CLFN Housing Department. No work beyond the approved SOW may commence without written amendment signed by the Housing Manager or Executive Director.

2. Licensing & Insurance

The contractor warrants that it holds all required trade licences, WSIB clearance, and liability insurance in force for the duration of the work. Expiry of any required coverage automatically suspends the right to work on CLFN property until coverage is reinstated and verified.

3. Invoicing & Payment

Invoices must reference the SOW number, unit address, and work completed. Payment is subject to satisfactory inspection by the Housing Manager. CLFN's standard payment terms are Net 30 days from invoice approval. Holdback provisions apply as per the Construction Act (Ontario).

4. On-Reserve Conduct

The contractor and all workers must conduct themselves respectfully on reserve lands. CLFN reserves the right to remove any individual from the reserve for misconduct. Alcohol, drugs, and firearms are prohibited on all work sites. All workers must check in with the Housing Department on first arrival.

5. Deficiency & Warranty

The contractor guarantees all labour and materials for a minimum of one (1) year from the date of completion. Any deficiencies identified during inspection must be rectified within 30 days at no additional cost to CLFN.

6. Indigenous Procurement Priority

CLFN is committed to economic reconciliation. Where qualified, Indigenous-owned businesses are given preference in contract awards. Classification provided on this form is subject to verification and must be accurate. Misrepresentation of Indigenous status may result in termination of the engagement and removal from the approved contractor list.

7. Termination

CLFN may terminate the engagement with 5 days written notice for convenience, or immediately for cause (including safety violations, misconduct, or material breach of these terms). The contractor is entitled to payment only for work satisfactorily completed to the date of termination.

Additional Notes
Terms & Conditions

Constance Lake First Nation — Housing Department

By completing and submitting this Scope of Work, the submitting party acknowledges and agrees to the following terms.

1. Prioritization of Requests

Renovation requests are assessed and prioritized based on the urgency of need, health and safety risk to occupants, and the overall condition of the unit. Requests involving immediate hazards — including structural, electrical, plumbing, or fire safety concerns — are given priority consideration over general maintenance and cosmetic work.

2. Funding Eligibility & Unit Qualifying Criteria

Approval is subject to available funding and the qualifying criteria of the unit under its applicable program (e.g. ISC, CMHC Section 95, CMHC Section 56.1, or Band-funded). Not all units qualify under all funding sources, and funding availability may affect the scope, cost ceiling, or timing of approved work. Requests will be assessed against the current budget allocation for the relevant funding pool.

3. Budget Authority & Approval Routing

Renovation requests within the Housing Manager's approved budget authority may be approved by the Housing Manager. Any request exceeding that threshold — as established in the Housing Department's current budget policy — requires Executive Director approval before work may commence. No work is to be initiated until all required approvals have been obtained and documented.

4. Tenant Responsibilities

The tenant is required to provide timely and reasonable access to the unit for inspection, assessment, and the completion of approved work. Damage, negligence, or vandalism attributed to the tenant may reduce the priority of the request and may result in the tenant being held financially responsible for a portion of repair costs, as determined by the Housing Manager.

5. No Guarantee of Approval or Timeline

Submission of this form does not guarantee approval or a specific completion date. The CLFN Housing Department will communicate decisions in writing. Priority and scheduling are subject to change based on available resources, contractor availability, and emerging urgent needs within the community.

6. Accuracy of Information

All information provided in this Scope of Work must be accurate and complete. Submitting false or misleading information may result in the request being cancelled, delayed, or referred for further review.

By signing below, the tenant and the housing staff member confirm they have read, understood, and agree to these terms.
